## What is Solsmint?

Solsmint is a no-code toolkit for the full life of a Solana token. Writing and deploying an SPL token by hand usually means installing the Solana CLI, managing keypairs, uploading metadata, and building each transaction yourself. Solsmint replaces that with a guided web interface, so creators, founders, communities, and developers can handle the most common token tasks in a few clicks.

With Solsmint you can:

- **Create** a Solana SPL token with a name, symbol, supply, decimals, logo, and description.
- **Decide on authorities** at launch by locking Mint Authority and disabling Freeze Authority if you want a fixed-supply, immutable token.
- **Mint** more supply later, as long as your wallet still holds Mint Authority.
- **Burn** tokens to permanently reduce circulating supply.
- **Airdrop** tokens to many wallets in batches.
- **Add liquidity** to Raydium pools so your token can be traded.
- **Mint NFTs** on Solana with your own artwork, metadata, and attributes.
- **Check token risk** with an on-chain rug checker before you trade or launch.
- **Track the market** with a live crypto bubble map.

Every action is signed in your own wallet. Solsmint never asks for your seed phrase or private key.

## How Solsmint works

[Mermaid diagram](https://render.pplxusercontent.com/mermaid)

1. **You stay in control.** Solsmint prepares the transactions, but nothing reaches the blockchain until you approve it in your wallet.
2. **Metadata comes first.** Your token logo and metadata are uploaded before the mint is created, so wallets and explorers can show your token's name, symbol, and image.
3. **Authorities decide what happens next.** If you keep Mint Authority, you can increase supply later. If you lock it, supply is fixed for good. Freeze Authority works the same way for account controls.
4. **After launch,** you manage the token from the same platform: mint, burn, airdrop, add liquidity, and check how your token looks to traders.

## How to create a Solana token step by step

1. Go to the [Solsmint Token Creator](https://www.solsmint.com/create).
2. Connect a Solana wallet such as **Phantom** or **Solflare**. Keep enough SOL in it to cover the displayed cost.
3. Enter your **token name**.
4. Enter your **symbol** (up to 8 characters, for example `$TICKER`).
5. Set **decimals**. Six is the standard default and the recommended value.
6. Enter the **total supply**, for example 1,000,000.
7. Add a short **description** and optional **social links**.
8. Upload your **token logo** (PNG recommended, up to 10 MB).
9. Choose your **authority settings**:
   - **Lock Mint Authority** if you want a fixed supply that can never grow.
   - **Disable Freeze Authority** if you want to give up the ability to freeze holder accounts.
10. Review everything, click **Create Token**, and approve the transaction in your wallet.
11. Save your **token mint address** and check it on a Solana explorer.

Want the full walkthrough? Read the [Solana Token Creation: Complete Guide for Beginners](https://blog.solsmint.com/blog/solana-token-creation-beginner-s-complete-guide).

- **Burn:** Connect the wallet that owns the tokens, select the token, choose an amount (25%, 50%, or Max), and approve. Burning is permanent. Keep the transaction signature as public proof of the burn.

## Add or remove liquidity on Raydium

Liquidity lets people trade your token. With Solsmint you choose a token pair, confirm both mint addresses, set your starting amounts, and approve. Liquidity is added directly to Raydium. Your starting ratio sets the opening price, so an unbalanced deposit can cause immediate price impact. Keep enough SOL for account creation, pool instructions, and network fees, and save the pool address after confirmation.

Liquidity providers face market risk, price impact, and impermanent loss. Solsmint does not charge a platform fee for liquidity actions.

## Check a token with the Solana Rug Checker

Paste a token's **mint address** (not a wallet or pool address) to review public on-chain signals:

- Whether Mint and Freeze Authority are still active
- Holder concentration, with program-owned and pool balances separated where possible
- Token-2022 extensions such as transfer fees, transfer hooks, and permanent delegates
- Detected DEX liquidity and pool information

When data can't be verified, the report labels it as unknown instead of marking it safe. A scan reflects one moment in time. Treat it as research, not a safety guarantee.

Explore: [Solana token rug check](https://www.solsmint.com/rug-check)

## Solana token basics you should understand first

| Term                 | What it means                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **SPL token**        | A fungible token on Solana created with the Solana Program Library token program. It's Solana's equivalent of an ERC-20 token on Ethereum.      |
| **Mint address**     | The unique on-chain address that identifies your token. Share it so people can find and verify the real token.                                  |
| **Mint Authority**   | The wallet allowed to create new supply. Revoking it fixes the supply permanently.                                                              |
| **Freeze Authority** | The wallet allowed to freeze token accounts. Many traders prefer tokens where this has been revoked.                                            |
| **Update Authority** | Controls changes to the token's metadata (name, symbol, image) under the metadata standard used. It is separate from Mint and Freeze Authority. |
| **Decimals**         | How many decimal places the token can be divided into. Six is a common default.                                                                 |
| **Token metadata**   | The name, symbol, logo, and description that wallets and explorers display.                                                                     |
| **Liquidity pool**   | A pool of two assets, such as SOL and your token, that allows trading. It does not guarantee buyers or a stable price.                          |
| **Token-2022**       | Solana's newer token program, which supports optional extensions like transfer fees and transfer hooks.                                         |

Revoking an authority usually cannot be undone. Decide on your settings before launch.

## Frequently asked questions

## What is Solsmint?

Solsmint is a web-based, no-code platform for creating and managing Solana SPL tokens. It includes a token creator, mint and burn tools, an airdrop tool, Raydium liquidity tools, an NFT minter, a rug checker, and a crypto market bubble map.

## How much does it cost to create a Solana token with Solsmint?

About **0.05 SOL in total**: a 0.03 SOL platform fee plus about 0.02 SOL in Solana network fees. The creator shows a live estimate before you sign, and the final amount appears in your wallet.

## Is minting or burning tokens free on Solsmint?

Yes. Solsmint charges no platform fee for minting or burning. You only pay the standard Solana network fee.

## Do I need to know how to code?

No. Solsmint handles the blockchain transactions for you. You still need to understand the settings you choose, especially supply, decimals, and authorities, and review every transaction before approving it.

## Which wallets work with Solsmint?

Phantom, Solflare, and other compatible Solana wallets.

## Can I revoke Mint Authority and Freeze Authority?

Yes. During token creation you can lock Mint Authority and disable Freeze Authority. This usually can't be reversed, so decide before you launch.

## Can I mint more tokens after launch?

Yes, but only if your wallet still holds the token's Mint Authority. If it has been revoked, nobody can increase the supply.

## Does Solsmint charge for airdrops?

No. Airdrops have no Solsmint service fee. The sender pays the Solana network fees.

## Can I add liquidity to Raydium through Solsmint?

Yes. Solsmint lets you create pools and add or remove liquidity on Raydium with no platform fee. Network fees and Raydium pool fees still apply.

## How do I check if a Solana token is a rug pull?

Paste the token's mint address into the [Solsmint Rug Checker](https://www.solsmint.com/rug-check). It reviews authorities, holder concentration, Token-2022 extensions, and liquidity. It's a research aid, not a guarantee.

## Does Solsmint have its own token?

No. Solsmint has not launched any official or platform token. Official announcements come only from the Solsmint website and its verified social channels.

## Does creating a token guarantee it will be listed or traded?

No. Token creation does not guarantee listings, trading activity, liquidity, price growth, or project success.
